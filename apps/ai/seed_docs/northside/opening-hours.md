# Opening hours

Synthetic demo content for Northside Family Clinic. This is not a real clinic.

## Weekday opening hours

Reception and the phone lines are open from 8am to 6.30pm, Monday to Friday.
The doors open at 7.45am for anyone with an early appointment.

Phone lines are busiest between 8am and 9.30am. If your call is not urgent, the
quietest time to reach us is mid-afternoon.

## Extended hours

We run an extended-hours clinic on Tuesday evenings until 8pm and on Saturday
mornings from 8.30am to noon. These appointments are pre-booked only — the
building is not open for walk-ins during extended hours.

## Weekends

The practice is closed on Sundays.

## Bank holidays

We are closed on all bank holidays. The Friday before and the Tuesday after a
bank holiday weekend are the busiest days of the month, so order repeat
prescriptions well before them.

## When we are closed

If you need medical help when the practice is closed, call 111. In an emergency,
call 999 or go to your nearest emergency department.

The answering machine gives these numbers, but it cannot take a message and
nobody listens to it out of hours.
