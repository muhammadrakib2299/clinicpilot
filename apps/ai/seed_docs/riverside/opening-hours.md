# Opening hours

Synthetic demo content for Riverside Medical Group. This is not a real clinic.

Deliberately different from Northside's. The two clinics are separate tenants,
and asking the same question of each should return each one's own answer — which
is what makes tenant isolation something a demo can show rather than assert.

## Weekday opening hours

Reception and the phone lines are open from 8.30am to 6pm, Monday to Thursday,
and from 8.30am to 4pm on Friday.

## Extended hours

We do not run evening clinics. Riverside patients can book weekend appointments
at the shared hub on Wharf Street, Saturday and Sunday, 9am to 5pm. Book through
reception; the hub does not take bookings directly.

## Weekends

The practice building is closed at weekends. The Wharf Street hub is open, and
the clinician there can see your record.

## Bank holidays

We are closed on all bank holidays, and the hub is closed on Christmas Day, Boxing
Day and New Year's Day.

## When we are closed

If you need medical help when the practice is closed, call 111. In an emergency,
call 999 or go to your nearest emergency department.
