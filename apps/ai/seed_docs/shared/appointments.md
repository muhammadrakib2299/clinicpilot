# Appointments

Synthetic demo content. This is not a real clinic and none of it is medical advice.

## Booking an appointment

You can book an appointment online, through the practice app, or by calling
reception. Routine appointments can usually be booked up to four weeks ahead.

If you need to be seen the same day, call as soon as the lines open. Same-day
appointments are released each morning and are kept for urgent problems.

## Cancelling an appointment

Please give at least 24 hours' notice if you cannot attend. You can cancel
online, in the app, or by replying to the reminder message we send you.

Cancelling lets us offer the slot to someone else. Around one in twenty
appointments is missed without notice, and each one is a slot nobody could use.

## Missed appointments

We do not charge for a missed appointment. If you miss three appointments in
twelve months without cancelling, we will write to you, and we may ask you to
book by phone rather than online for a while.

## Running late

If you arrive more than ten minutes after your appointment time we may not be
able to see you, because it pushes every appointment after yours back. Call us
if you are delayed and we will tell you whether the clinician can still fit
you in.

## Length of appointments

A standard appointment is ten minutes and covers one problem. If you have
several things to discuss, book a double appointment when you make the booking
rather than at the desk on the day.

## Home visits

Home visits are for patients who cannot leave home because of their condition.
Request one by calling before 10am if possible, so the clinician can plan the
round. A home visit is not usually a faster way to be seen.

## Bringing someone with you

You are welcome to bring a relative, friend or carer. Tell reception if you
would like them to be present during the consultation.
