# Registering and your records

Synthetic demo content. This is not a real clinic and none of it is medical advice.

## Registering with the practice

Anyone living in the practice area can register. Fill in the form online or at
reception. You do not need proof of address or immigration status to register,
though bringing photo identification and a recent letter makes it quicker.

Registration usually takes five working days. You can be seen before it
completes if you need to be.

## Changing your address or phone number

Tell us as soon as either changes. Appointment reminders and result
notifications go to the number on your record, and the commonest reason someone
misses one is a number we were never given.

## Seeing your medical records

You can see most of your record in the practice app, including test results,
allergies, medications and recent letters.

For anything not in the app, or for a copy of your full record, make a subject
access request at reception or in writing. We will respond within one month.
There is no charge unless a request is repeated or excessive.

## Correcting something in your record

If something in your record is wrong, tell us and we will look at it. Factual
errors are corrected. Where a clinical opinion is disputed, we add your comment
alongside it rather than deleting what was written.

## Who can see your record

Your record is shared with clinicians involved in your care. It is not shared
for any other purpose without your consent, except where the law requires it.
You can opt out of the national data sharing programmes; ask reception for the
form.

## Making a complaint

Speak to the practice manager first, in person, by phone or in writing. Most
things are resolved this way. If you are not satisfied, the letter we send will
tell you who to escalate to and how long you have to do it.
