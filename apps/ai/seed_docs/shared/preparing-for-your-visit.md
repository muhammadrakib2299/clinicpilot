# Preparing for your visit

Synthetic demo content. This is not a real clinic and none of it is medical advice.

## What to bring

Bring a list of every medication you take, including anything bought without a
prescription, and any vitamins or supplements. Bring your reading glasses if you
need them for forms.

If you are new to the practice, bring photo identification and something showing
your current address.

## Fasting before a blood test

Some blood tests need you to fast. If yours does, the letter or message we send
will say so, and it will say for how long — usually eight to twelve hours.

Fasting means no food and no drinks other than plain water. Keep taking your
usual medication unless a clinician has told you otherwise. Fasting appointments
are offered first thing in the morning so the wait is short.

If you are unsure whether your test needs fasting, call before the appointment
rather than fasting unnecessarily.

## Bringing a urine sample

If you have been asked for a urine sample, collect it in a sterile container
from reception or a pharmacy. A clean jam jar is not suitable. Bring it in the
same day, ideally within two hours.

## Interpreters

We can book a telephone or in-person interpreter free of charge, in most
languages and in British Sign Language. Ask when you book, because an
interpreter needs to be arranged in advance. Family members are welcome, but we
prefer a professional interpreter for anything clinical.

## Accessibility

The building is step-free throughout and there is an accessible toilet on the
ground floor. There is a hearing loop at reception and in every consulting room.
Tell us if you need a longer appointment, a ground-floor room, or a quiet space
to wait in, and we will note it on your record.

## Parking

There are twelve spaces behind the building, including two accessible bays. They
fill quickly at the start of each session. There is on-street parking on the
surrounding roads and a bus stop directly outside.

## Chaperones

You can ask for a chaperone for any examination, at any time, and it will not
affect your care. If none is available we will offer to rebook you at a time
when one is.
