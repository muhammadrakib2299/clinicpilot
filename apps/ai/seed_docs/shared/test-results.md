# Test results

Synthetic demo content. This is not a real clinic and none of it is medical advice.

## How to get your results

Results appear in the practice app when they have been reviewed by a clinician.
You can also call reception after 2pm, when the phones are quieter.

We cannot give results by message or email, and reception staff cannot interpret
them. If a result needs explaining, we will book you a call with a clinician.

## How long results take

Blood tests usually come back within three to five working days. X-rays take up
to two weeks. Some specialist tests take longer, and the clinician who ordered
the test will tell you what to expect.

A result is not ready until it has been reviewed. An unreviewed number is not an
answer, which is why the app does not show one.

## If a result is abnormal

We will contact you if a result needs action. We aim to do that within two
working days of the result being reviewed.

Not every abnormal result is serious, and many need nothing more than a repeat
test. That conversation happens with a clinician rather than by message.

## If we do not contact you

Please still check your results rather than assuming no news is good news. If
you have not heard anything and your result is not in the app a week after the
expected date, call and ask.

## Results for someone else

We cannot give results to anyone but the patient without written consent, or
proof of parental responsibility or a lasting power of attorney. Bring the
paperwork to reception and we will record it.
