# Repeat prescriptions

Synthetic demo content. This is not a real clinic and none of it is medical advice.

## Ordering a repeat prescription

Order online, through the practice app, or by handing in the tear-off slip from
your last prescription. We do not take prescription requests over the phone,
because a misheard drug name is a dangerous mistake.

## How long a repeat prescription takes

Allow two full working days, not counting weekends and bank holidays. Order
before you run out rather than on the day of your last dose. During bank holiday
weeks, order earlier.

## Where your prescription goes

Prescriptions are sent electronically to the pharmacy you have nominated. You
can change your nominated pharmacy at any time by telling reception or the
pharmacy itself. If you have not nominated one, the prescription waits at the
practice for collection.

## Running out of medication

If you have run out and cannot wait two days, contact the practice and explain.
An urgent request is possible but it interrupts a clinician's list, so it is for
genuine emergencies rather than a forgotten order.

## Medication reviews

Most repeat medications need a review at least once a year. We will contact you
when yours is due. If a review is overdue, the practice may issue a short
prescription rather than a full one until the review has happened.

## Controlled drugs

Some medications are controlled drugs and cannot be issued early or in larger
quantities. These are dispensed for a limited period at a time and the pharmacy
may ask for identification when you collect them.

## Changes to your medication

Never change a dose or stop a medication because of anything you read here. If
something is not working, or you are having side effects, book a review and
speak to a clinician.
